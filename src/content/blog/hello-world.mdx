I'm Emmanuel Alabi. Most people online know me as **Alabsgold**. I'm a Computer Science student at Joseph Ayo Babalola University and the founder of [ALABSGOLD](https://alabsgold.vercel.app), a small web engineering studio.

## What ALABSGOLD does

We build software for businesses whose next customer is on another continent: exporters, food importers, real-estate operators and diaspora brands. Those businesses often lose deals to a credibility gap rather than to a weaker product. A slow site, a broken quotation flow or an unbranded email is enough for an overseas buyer to walk away.

So we hand-build the things that close that gap: fast catalogues, ordering flows, admin panels the client can run themselves, and payment systems that verify themselves.
